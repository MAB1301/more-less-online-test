# Second research expansion — original graphics

Twenty-one original SVG illustrations accompany the second data expansion: twelve element-symbol tiles, three sports-equipment symbols, three mission launch symbols and three park landscape symbols. They are released under CC0-1.0 and are rebuilt with `python scripts/build-expansion-visuals.py`.

No asset contains a numeric quiz solution. Mission graphics illustrate the launch theme; they do not depict the actual spacecraft or launch vehicle. Park graphics are free landscape symbols, not documentary photographs or geographic diagrams. Existing photographs retain the attribution and license documented in [the image credits](../credits.html).
