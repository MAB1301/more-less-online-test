# Medium priorities — 2026-10-04

This release adds the eight Medium items across games, results, site and content operations.

- Signed-in question recency for the four Solo/local games is loaded before selection and written under the caller's auth.uid. More/Less Online host selection uses that recency too. Fact/Fake Online selects a shared sourced, balanced 10-question pool, preferring questions unseen by its roster. Guest history and fixed-account histories remain separate. Exhausted pools can repeat; this prefers fresh questions, it does not promise unlimited uniqueness or reserve questions across simultaneous sessions.
- Fact/Fake Online keeps hidden answers on the server, commits each player's first boolean answer (including false), reveals after all roster answers or 60 seconds, awards each correct answer once, and lets only the host advance/reset. Late joiners spectate until a new match.
- Results show actual hit rate, best correct streak and a comparable previous local attempt for all four local games, More/Less Online and Fact/Fake Online. The previous-attempt comparison uses this device's record for the selected account. Online Jeopardy remains a shared team score, and estimate standings retain their numeric error summaries.
- The account menu opens cloud personal records and completed Daily progress. Solo client records are personal, not trusted global scores. Guests retain local Solo records and their separate Daily identity. Failed account writes are retried during the current session when the profile is opened; local records survive reload.
- Additional day/week rankings support global and accepted-friend views, tied dense ranks and pagination. Periods use Berlin dates and only completions before that Daily day's end. Existing archive Daily leaderboards remain available; late archive completions do not rewrite settled rewards.
- Positive ranks 1–3 receive cosmetic gold/silver/bronze badges after each day and completed week; ties receive equal badges. The best displayed badge also supplies a profile frame. Rewards award no score advantages.
- A reproducible source review schedule covers reviewed catalogue/trivia records. Volatile records have a 90-day review interval, stable/historical records 365 days. Existing source verification dates are preserved. Edition/year-specific figures retain their edition. No source is silently re-approved by a scheduled date. The account's Sources & Review plan opens public source metadata, not private analytics.
- A weekly automation prepares small sourced and validated content packages in separate Draft PRs, without changing live Supabase or merging automatically. It checks current content and open PRs, records actual source/date/year, and uses 4:3 card / 16:9 detail visuals. Target: 10 comparisons, 5 estimates, 5 facts and 5 Jeopardy clues per package; fewer when verification needs it. Mondays morning, Europe/Berlin. Created successfully in this conversation.
